A full adder is a fundamental digital combinational logic circuit that adds three 1-bit binary numbers (two main inputs \(A\) and \(B\), plus a carry-in \(C_{in}\)) and produces two 1-bit outputs: Sum (\(S\)) and Carry-out (\(C_{out}\)).
